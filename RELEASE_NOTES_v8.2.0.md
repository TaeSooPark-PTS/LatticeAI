# Lattice AI v8.2.0 Release Notes

Brain Brief release.

## Highlights

- Brain Home now includes a compact Brain Brief that tells users what to notice,
  what real evidence supports it, and what to do next.
- The brief is generated by `MemoryService` from actual workspace memory,
  conversation, graph, vector, and source-health signals.
- Empty Brain states stay honest: Lattice suggests adding a source or asking the
  first question without claiming model-continuity proof.
- Recall-backed Brain states offer direct actions for asking, inspecting graph
  links, verifying model-independent proof, and managing backups.
- Model loading/runtime bodies and WorkspaceOS graph trace/run/skill ownership
  continue moving into focused modules behind compatibility facades.
