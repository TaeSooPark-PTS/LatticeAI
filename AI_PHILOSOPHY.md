# AI Philosophy

Lattice AI's AI philosophy is simple: the user should not work for AI. AI should
work for the user.

## What That Means

- Users can provide PDFs, spreadsheets, images, screenshots, notes, and chats as
  they are.
- AI should read, organize, connect, and explain.
- The system should disclose sources and risks in normal language.
- A recommendation should include a reason, not just a button.
- A limitation should include why it exists and what the user can do next.

## Protection Without Control

Lattice AI should protect users by:

- showing where a model comes from
- showing whether internet access is needed
- showing whether files may leave the computer
- explaining risky or experimental actions
- keeping admin powers auditable

It should not protect users by silently removing choices or hiding why a choice
is unavailable.
