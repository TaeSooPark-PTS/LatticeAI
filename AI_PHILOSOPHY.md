# AI Philosophy

Lattice AI's AI philosophy is simple: models are temporary, but the user's
knowledge is durable. The user should not work for AI. AI should work for the
user's Brain.

The Brain is the product. Notes, documents, conversations, decisions, projects,
relationships, and context are the durable asset. Models can be replaced,
upgraded, unloaded, or routed differently without making the Brain disposable.

## What That Means

- Users can provide PDFs, spreadsheets, images, screenshots, notes, and chats as
  they are.
- AI should read, organize, connect, and explain.
- Conversation is the primary interaction with the Brain.
- Memories, knowledge, relationships, and the graph are progressive layers, not
  the default home screen.
- The system should disclose sources and risks in normal language.
- A recommendation should include a reason, not just a button.
- A limitation should include why it exists and what the user can do next.

## Protection Without Control

Lattice AI should protect users by:

- showing where a model comes from
- showing whether internet access is needed
- showing whether files may leave the computer
- explaining risky or experimental actions
- keeping admin powers auditable
- preserving backup, restore, export, and portable `.latticebrain` ownership

It should not protect users by silently removing choices or hiding why a choice
is unavailable.
