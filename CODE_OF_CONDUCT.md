# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Lattice AI community to be open, respectful, and useful for people with different backgrounds, skill levels, and viewpoints.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a healthy community:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Giving and receiving feedback in good faith
- Assuming curiosity before hostility
- Focusing critiques on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spam, malicious content, or intentionally disruptive behavior

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, release notes, and direct interactions that clearly represent the project community.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer using the reporting channel listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, including warnings, content removal, temporary bans, or permanent bans from community spaces.
